# Observatory Camp

The supplied 41 × 24 × 27 observatory generates only in Snowy Slopes and Grove. It uses the rare Woodland Mansion placement scale: 80-chunk spacing, 20-chunk separation and triangular spread. Nearby candidate sites are sampled to fit mountain terrain; the existing twelve-block slope budget rejects unsuitable locations. Its complete foundation aligns with the median sampled surface height, and a five-block snowy clearing blends the ground around it. The final decoration pass protects the building from trees and terrain decoration without repeatedly overwriting player edits.

The template preserves every authored block, password, loot table, journal page and note. Editor capability snapshots are removed. The source contains no entities, so the import adds one Eagle Owl, two Yuins, one Blue Wolf and one Pink Wyvern at collision-free positions. All belong to the Mountain population and use the existing natural-camp routines, storage reactions and home boundaries.

The **Observatory Archivist** is the fixed Eagle Owl Chief: curious and polite, with orange (`#F59A23`) Original-style eyes and the Aviator Outfit. He starts in his downstairs bedroom beside the lectern journal, at template-local `(5.5, 2, 14.5)`. His clothing, stable identity, conversations, enrolment routes and twenty-minute return use the same systems as the other fixed NPCs. He sleeps by day; the built-in perches are available through the camp's normal bed search.

Dialogue uses the revised Observatory Camp tree: observations, nocturnal habits, the visitors from the tents, practical hospitality and practiced human speech. Human and transformed visitors receive different greetings. Mental lines retain the existing understanding checks. The draft's unconfirmed Dust cosmology and orbital-tether history are not added. Enrolment belongs to this individual camp, with independent local trust and 0/5/25/35 admission rewards. A permanent ritual requires a separate character-spoken confirmation and supported TrueTransfur backend.

Each fixed NPC's third background topic requires completing the first two topics with that individual. Completion is stored per player and NPC, including when the player finishes a topic with Goodbye. Closing halfway through does not complete it. The server filters and revalidates locked choices; progress survives saves and NPC returns.

The treehouse's edited ground-level hive now contains three persistent Latex Bees with randomized native eyes. Other treehouse blocks, stories and residents are unchanged. Existing instances receive these preset changes on their next entity load without replacing their identities or inventories.
