# Faction Reputation and Alliances

Faction standing is separate from personal friendship and creature bonds. **Respect begins at 40** and provides friendly access and protection benefits. **Alliance begins at 70** and adds faction support and allied privileges.

## Rival factions

Rivalry describes political competition, not an automatic reason to attack. Dark and White latex are neutral toward one another by default, including NPCs, transformed players, native players and visible TrueTransfur Instinct bodies. They keep separate factions, territories and wild communities. Actual aggression, theft, betrayal and hostile reputation still follow the usual defense and reconciliation rules. Peace does not grant friendship or access to another community's supplies.

By default, allying with one faction can reduce your standing with its rival:

| Faction | Rival |
| --- | --- |
| Dark | White |
| Mountain | Cave |
| Aquatic | Desert |
| Forest | Badlands |
| Taiga | Jungle |
| Swamp | Savanna |
| Plains | Snowy |

Other pairs have no automatic reputation penalty. These rivalries do not prevent mixed-species camps.

- Gains below Alliance do not reduce another faction's reputation.
- Reaching Alliance with one faction ends an existing alliance with its rival, bringing the rival to at most 69.
- Further gains can lower the rival's standing: an alliance score of 80 can bring it to 64, and 100 to 54. Linked losses alone do not reduce it below Respect.
- You can rebuild the rival through peaceful interactions. Reaching Alliance with it switches sides; no attack is needed.
- Attacking a faction does not earn reputation with its rivals.

You can be friends with every faction and maintain multiple alliances that do not conflict. Previously earned advancements are retained when an alliance ends.

## Friends in Every Corner

Keep **40 or more** reputation with White, Dark, Aquatic and all 14 regional variant groups at the same time:

Cave, Taiga, Swamp, Jungle, Savanna, Desert, Badlands, Snowy, Mountain, Beach, River, Ocean, Forest and Plains.

This advancement requires friendship, not an alliance with every faction. Individual alliance advancements are earned at 70.

## Server option

**Independent Faction Reputation** is off by default. Enable it to let every faction's reputation grow independently, allowing rival alliances to coexist:

```mcfunction
/changedsynergy config set RELATIONSHIPS.IndependentFactionReputation true
```

Previously lost reputation is not refunded. Turning the setting off resolves incompatible rival alliances. For other faction controls, see the [settings guide](SETTINGS_GUIDE.md).
