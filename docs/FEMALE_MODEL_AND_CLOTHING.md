# Synergy female models and clothing

Synergy's female forms use long hair, a segmented curved torso and a flat chest. Their arms are three pixels wide, except Spotted Hyena, which keeps the supplied sturdy four-pixel limbs, mane and body. Do not add chest protrusions to their entity, transfur-helper, armor or clothing models.

The current female Muntjac and Hyena models were imported from the author's live Blockbench projects on 2026-11-02. The Hyena's non-exported eye-layout preview is deliberately excluded. Male models are unchanged.

`HyenaTrailclothItem.synergyFemale` resolves the actual Changed body for NPCs, transformed players and wrapped players, then limits the new cut to Synergy's own female entity types. `female` retains its original meaning. Changed and addon female forms continue using the original feminine Trailcloth and Canopy cuts; Aviator has a separate fitted chest panel for original Changed/addon female forms, preventing the old flat vest from clipping through them.

The new cuts use the existing 128×128 material atlases and limb fitting. Canopy and Aviator contour their torso sections to the new waist. Female Hyena Trailcloth keeps the original brown/orange palette, arm wraps, belt, clasp and hip pouch, with wide shoulder straps and a short upper garment leaving room for the mane. Slender Synergy females use a separate flat, fitted Trailcloth cut.

`SynergyFemaleArmor` supplies the native Changed armor model set used by shirts, lab coats and other standard clothing. Sleeves retain the standard four-pixel UV layout, with a static three-quarter width/depth scale for slim arms. The original clothing inventory, inner/outer slots, conflicting slots, style interactions, sounds and Curios integration remain authoritative.

Editable outfit derivatives are generated by `tools/adapt_synergy_female_outfits.py`. It never overwrites the original projects or legacy runtime layers. The current derivatives have 10/12/20/25 cubes for slender Trailcloth, Hyena Trailcloth, Canopy and Aviator respectively, using one existing material per outfit. The legacy female Aviator cut adds three chest panels (27 cubes total). Tail/legless/taur lower-section restrictions and juvenile headwear behavior are unchanged.

The client regression `FemaleStyleClientChecks` exercises the two native female body types and a legacy female wolf with all three outfits, a native T-shirt and lab coat, including first-person sleeves, arm poses and the native coat interaction. It produces front/back comparison galleries. It does not establish compatibility with every third-party clothing renderer that supplies its own model instead of Changed's native model set.

Interior caps at segmented torso seams are omitted from native clothing and the three fitted outfit cuts. This prevents opaque trouser UVs appearing as a horizontal shelf. Trailcloth overlaps the structural body transition, while the female Hyena uses a dedicated copy of the original atlas with the ivory stitching moved from the seam to the upper edge. Legacy feminine materials remain unchanged. The Synergy Canopy ribbon matches the male ribbon bounds so its inner gold end face stays inside the head.
