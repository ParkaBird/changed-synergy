# Latex Jerboa / 胶液跳鼠

The Jerboa is a small, quick desert species in Changed: Synergy 1.1.5-Beta.2. Its body is fitted at 80% of the usual humanoid scale, with responsive ears and matching clothing and armor.

The attribute values are native Changed attributes, not blocks per second or a promised jump height. Natural spawns use deserts, badlands and wooded badlands, with weight 4 and groups of one or two, subject to normal spawn conditions. The species has a Native Origin record and matching transfur items. Native identity still requires the supported TrueTransfur integration.

## Charged jump

Tap and release jump for a normal jump, or hold it to charge a higher one. While charging, a horse-style meter temporarily replaces the experience bar. Muntjacs and Jerboas share this passive, including when controlling a compatible wrapped body. Entering an incompatible state cancels the charge.

Landing protection covers the charged jump's own rise. Jumping from a high ledge can still cause damage from the extra fall. See [Muntjac](LATEX_MUNTJAC.md) for the shared input timing.

## Outfit and community

The [Wayfarer Outfit](WAYFARER_OUTFIT.md) automatically selects its Pathfinder cut on a Jerboa; this is the same item used by other supported bodies. The Desert Refuge's Pathfinder is a fixed Jerboa Chief. Ordinary Jerboas may also live in Oasis and Muddy camps. Household admission and local trust are explained in the [community guide](CAMP_MEMBERSHIP_GUIDE.md).

## Drops

The species loot table includes rabbit hide, wheat seeds, a player-kill rare rabbit-foot roll and the common white-latex pool for white latex goo and latex base. Quantities and rare drops use their normal Looting rules. Zero is a valid result for several rolls, so a single kill need not drop every item. Fixed NPC equipment and return rules remain separate from ordinary species loot.
