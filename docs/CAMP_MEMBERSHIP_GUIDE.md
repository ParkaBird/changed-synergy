# Camp Community and Membership Guide

For **Changed: Synergy 1.1.5-Beta.2**. This guide covers community membership, contributions and everyday play. Server settings can affect which features are available.

## Your first visit

1. Find a Forest Treehouse, Hyena's Hideout, Observatory or Desert Refuge and choose **Dialogue** with its Chief.
2. Hear about the household and ask to stay. You can remain human.
3. Open the relationship roster and meet the residents marked as campmates.
4. Ask how things are going. Hold supplies in your main hand and offer them through dialogue.
5. Try a request, checking whether it belongs to the community or an individual.
6. Share a meal, add sleeping places, tend crops and get to know your neighbors.

There is no daily attendance requirement or penalty for declining requests, exploring elsewhere or taking a break. Transformation is optional.

## Communities, outposts and factions

| Place or relationship | Your role |
| --- | --- |
| Naturally generated community | The building and residents already live here; ask the Chief to join their household |
| Player outpost | A base you establish and manage using its existing companion and binding systems |
| Wild creature outpost or smaller camp | Keeps its own routines, trading and storage rules; formal admission is not necessarily available |
| Regional faction | Wider reputation and diplomatic relations, recorded separately from a household's local trust |

Formal community admission currently covers **Forest Treehouses, Hyena's Hideouts, Observatories and Desert Refuges**. Joining one treehouse makes you part of that household, rather than every forest creature's campmate. Small hyena camps, Oasis camps, Muddy Campsites and laboratories do not provide this same admission system.

## Find the household and its Chief

| Community | Biomes | Chief | Local-trust outfit reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Forest Treehouse | Forest | Weaver (Silk Moth) | Ranger Outfit |
| Hyena's Hideout | Savanna | Matriarch (Spotted Hyena) | Trailcloth Outfit |
| Observatory | Snowy Slopes and Grove | Archivist (Eagle Owl) | Aviator Outfit |
| Desert Refuge | Desert | Pathfinder (Jerboa) | Wayfarer Outfit |

These are rare landmarks. Explorer maps can appear in selected related loot chests. Forest watchtowers can provide leads; tropical hyena camps can point toward a hideout, and mountain-related chests can hold observatory maps. Oasis camps, Muddy Campsites and selected desert or badlands loot chests can provide a map to a nearby refuge. A map is not guaranteed in every chest.

The green laboratory's Researcher also has individual conversations about his experiences, experiments and everyday mishaps. He is not a Chief and does not recruit members. He follows his own rest routine and can eat food he carries without taking laboratory loot.

The Weaver is calm and polite, the Matriarch protective and polite, and the Archivist curious and polite. The Pathfinder is cautious and polite. Their books, notes and clipboards add context to their stories. The third, more detailed background topic unlocks after the first two topics are completed.

## Conversation controls

Approach a character and choose **Dialogue** from the social wheel. The Chiefs discuss both their stories and household business. Talking to ordinary campmates also opens the community conversation instead of the usual friend or bond small talk.

Spoken words appear progressively. If your existing abilities let you understand private thoughts, those thoughts appear alongside the speech with a strike line and whole-sentence fade. Conversation does not itself unlock telepathy. All four Chiefs have learned human speech, so human visitors can understand admission and requests.

Click or press Enter to reveal the current passage, then continue. Control hints and replies appear together after the passage is complete. Use the arrow keys and Enter for replies, or Esc to leave. The world keeps running; combat or moving too far away can end a conversation. Each NPC speaks with one player at a time.

## Joining and choosing a body

You can apply with **local trust of 0 or higher**, no current hostility and a Chief who is not handling a fight. You can register one home at a time.

| Admission route | Body and identity | Admission trust allowance for this home |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stay human | Keep your body and origin | 0 |
| Keep an existing latex form | Keep your current body | 2 |
| Reversible ritual | Receive the species the Chief offers; this explicitly chosen ritual remains eligible for reversal | 5 |
| TT permanent ritual | Adopt that species as a native latex identity, using Native Origin's body rules | 8 |

A reversible ritual does not change wilderness transformation rules or make an already-permanent body reversible. You still need an available, permitted reversal method. Existing transformation, wrapping, takeover or a native body can make ritual options unavailable; joining as you are remains an option.

**Permanent rituals require TrueTransfur.** The Chief explains the consequences and asks for a second confirmation. Success plays the normal transformation sound. Ordinary reversal, including `/untf` and `/untransfur`, is intercepted before removal; you do not turn human and then snap back. Leaving the community does not remove the native body.

To become human again, use an authorized **Native Origin re-selection**. An administrator can open the archive with `/changedsynergy origin choose <player>`, subject to the existing permission and enabled Native Origins gamerule. Confirming human safely removes the native body; cancelling preserves it. Camp membership, local trust, relationships and reward history remain. `/changedsynergy origin` shows your current identity. Ordinary admission needs no administrator command.

Admission rewards top up the highest allowance already earned in that household. For example, someone who joined as human and later qualifies for a reversible ritual can receive the unpaid difference up to 5. Leaving and rejoining cannot repeat a reward. Older permanent-ritual body records migrate to Native Origin without paying admission rewards again.

## Four separate kinds of relationship

| Record | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Local trust | Standing with this particular household, governing shared privileges and community rewards |
| Individual affection | Your personal relationship with one resident |
| Bond | The existing companion relationship, with its normal eligibility and interactions |
| Regional faction reputation | Wider diplomatic standing, separate from local household trust |

Joining introduces the authored residents and adds **one affection point per individual**, once. Unloaded residents receive the introduction later when they load and you are online. The roster marks current campmates separately from bonds; both badges appear when both relationships apply.

Ordinary residents can be campmates, friends and bonded companions at the same time. An explicit following order takes priority; once home and free to act, they can resume community routines. Fixed Chiefs retain affection interactions but cannot become bonded followers or have their bodies taken over.

## Public supplies, private belongings and story media

Each formal community has a designated **Community Supplies** double chest. Shared food, gathering and hunting proceeds, picked-up items and player contributions use this reserve. Opening a loot chest does not convert it into a public reserve.

Members in good standing can share their own community's reserve. Residents take public supplies only from this chest and deposit their activity proceeds there. If it is full or unavailable, they keep what they carry instead of using someone else's chest.

Marked personal chests and other private storage remain private even to campmates. Nearby awake residents who can see the search will warn you; repeated searching in a short period can cost local trust, with a cooldown. **Searching these private containers alone does not start a storage-defence attack.** Breaking containers or attacking residents is a separate offence.

Lecterns and other readable story media are open to visitors without access penalties merely for reading. Items deliberately stored in a private container remain subject to that container's privacy, and NPC default clothing is not communal property. Unauthorized access to other protected stores, destruction and wild-outpost offences can still provoke defence.

## Ask how things are going

The Chief can explain food supplies, free sleeping places, safety, requests and your membership privileges. Residents may approach and call for your attention without forcing a screen open. If you cannot understand an ordinary resident, ask the Chief about household affairs.

### Share supplies

Hold the item in your main hand, choose the donation reply and confirm the amount. Suitable ordinary food, wool, leather, paper and torches are accepted when needed; the dialogue shows the current requirement. The server checks the item, quantity and destination space before accepting it. Custom-named items and bound NPC clothing are excluded from ordinary donations.

Putting items directly into the reserve can replenish it. Use the relevant conversation to register a contribution or fulfill a hand-in request. Supplies you borrowed and return are credited as returns before they can count as new donations.

### Public and personal requests

| Request | Who receives the hand-in | Destination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public supplies | Chief | Community Supplies chest |
| Personal supplies | The resident who asked | That resident's own inventory |
| Protection | Help against a real threat, then report once the camp is safe | Actual assistance evidence |

Treehouses commonly request wool, hideouts leather and observatories paper. A food shortage can also create a food request. Hold the complete requested amount for a hand-in. Personal requests do not need room in the public chest.

Public requests belong to the whole household; personal requests belong to the named resident. Another player may finish first. Current requests have no deadline, and reading one does not reserve it. Completed requests can refresh on a later active game day. Declining or postponing one carries no penalty.

Protection means helping residents against actual danger. Wait until things are safe before reporting. You do not need to lure enemies into camp, and authored livestock is not hunting prey.

### Build local trust

| Contribution | Usual trust gain |
| --- | --- |
| A useful new donation | +1 |
| A completed supply request | +2 |
| Harvesting mature crops and promptly replanting in place, witnessed by a resident | +1 |
| Actually helping protect residents | +2 |

These contribution rewards have a limit of **8 trust per player, per household, per server game-time day**. Sleeping through a night or logging out does not separately reset it. Reporting protection does not pay the same trust reward a second time. Affection and trust are separate scores.

Members in good standing with **40 local trust** can ask their Chief for the household outfit. Each player can claim it once per household; leaving and rejoining does not reset the claim. An inventory-space failure does not consume it.

## Everyday life

Residents gather and pick up supplies, harvest and replant crops, hunt nearby wild prey, eat and sleep around their home. They protect authored livestock and do not leave to establish another outpost. Ordinary residents have a small activity margin of roughly six blocks outside the building bounds; Chiefs retain their building boundaries.

Members can invite the household to a meal through dialogue. When the camp is safe, suitable food is available and its meal point is usable, available residents can gather. Communities with campfires require them to be lit; the Desert Refuge uses a designated gathering point without a campfire. You choose whether to join them; the game does not teleport you into a seat. Residents prefer usable seats, then carpets, then ordinary open ground.

Residents look for reachable, suitable, unoccupied sleeping places inside the structure, including beds placed later by players. Eligible bird residents prefer usable perches: one place on a stand, or one at each end of a rail. Eagle Owls sleep by day and are active at night; other species keep their own schedules. Unassigned wild creatures do not acquire a free-roaming community bed search.

Members may use suitable free sleeping places without displacing occupants. **Visitors with 40 local trust** can also borrow one. Each bed or perch retains its own body and sleep requirements. Player outposts can bind perches using their existing bed-binding mechanism too.

## Repairs and furnishing

- Once a public reserve has been registered, replacing ordinary chests at its **original two positions** restores recognition when they form a usable double chest. Lost contents are not recreated.
- Sleeping places do not have to come from the authored template. Add or replace beds, or place perches for eligible birds inside the structure. Leave clear routes and enough body space.
- Residents can use ordinary doors, wooden trapdoors and fence gates along their route and close them afterward. Iron trapdoors still need redstone.
- Template updates to chest positions, private markers and beds do not overwrite already-generated buildings. Older registered reserves retain their own repair positions; moving a chest elsewhere does not automatically move its registration.

## Moving home, leaving and losing privileges

Applying to another Chief first asks you to confirm a home change. Your old membership remains until confirmation, and you can have one registered home at a time. A permanent ritual still requires its own second confirmation.

To leave, ask the Chief about your place in the household and confirm. The campmate badge and home-member privileges end, while personal relationships, bonds, local trust, admission reward history and your current body remain. You can return as a visitor or help later. Trust is not copied between communities when you move.

At **−15 local trust**, member privileges are suspended until neutrality returns. At **−40**, membership ends. Harming residents, repeatedly invading privacy or damaging community property can reduce standing. Declining requests or traveling elsewhere does not cause those penalties.

## When something does not happen

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| No admission reply | Is this one of the four Chiefs? Are they in combat, hostile, or is your local trust below 0? |
| No permanent ritual | Is a supported TT backend installed? Are you already transformed, native, wrapped or under takeover? |
| Want to become human | Use authorized Native Origin re-selection; ordinary untf cannot remove native identity |
| Hand-in fails | Main-hand item and full quantity, request completion, and space in the public chest or recipient's inventory |
| Residents do not eat or sleep | Safety, food, usable meal point, species schedule, empty suitable places, paths and active follow/task orders |
| Reserve is not recognized | Its registered original position and both chest halves; an arbitrary new chest is not automatically public storage |
| Chief seems missing | Check their home and activity area; an unloaded chunk does not count as death or create a duplicate Chief |

After confirmed death or genuine disappearance, fixed characters normally become eligible to return after about **20 minutes of server game time**. Their home must be loaded and a safe position available; recovery does not force chunks to load. Relationships and story progress persist. Default clothes cannot be repeatedly farmed from returns.

Keep server and client versions aligned. Available features and permissions follow the server's configuration and installed mods.

## Smaller camps

Oasis camps, Muddy Campsites and the three smaller Hyena camps have their own residents, reserves and daily routines. Joining a refuge does not grant membership or remote access to their storage. Their built-in barrels hold resident supplies and are private to players; no extra reserve chests are added. The open-country Hyena camp uses its built-in chest for the same purpose. Initial loot is retained, and residents can use and replenish those supplies. Each small-camp resident has a one-time 25% chance to wear the camp outfit.

## Server settings

Community dialogue, routines, friendship, telepathy and Native Origin features follow the server's settings. No Civilization Mode temporarily evaluates regional reputation and local trust as zero and pauses their changes, while retaining stored records. Trust-based rewards cannot be earned in that state; personal friendship and bonds remain available. Ask the server operator when an otherwise eligible option is unavailable. Ordinary players do not need management commands to join or help a community.

For a camp you create and manage yourself, use the [player-camp guide](PLAYER_CAMP_GUIDE.md). See [server configuration](SERVER_CONFIGURATION.md) for settings and commands.
