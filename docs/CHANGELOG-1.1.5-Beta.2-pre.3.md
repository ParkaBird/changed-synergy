# Changed: Synergy 1.1.5-Beta.2-pre.3

Changes since **1.1.5-Beta.2-pre.2**.

### Permanent transformation

- Confirming a Chief's permanent-transformation dialogue now registers the player as a **Native Origin latex creature**. Ordinary reversal, including `/untf` and `/untransfur`, is blocked before the body is removed, rather than reversing it and restoring it afterward.
- Fixed human re-selection through Native Origin failing after a permanent ritual. Confirming human safely clears the permanent body; cancelling keeps it. Camp membership, local trust, relationships and admission reward history are retained.
- Existing permanent-ritual records migrate to Native Origin, preserving the original species and latest permanent form. Native Origin now owns body recovery, with safeguards for temporary suits, Instinct control and TT rebirth.

### Community dialogue and guides

- Broadened storage warnings in English, Chinese and Russian to refer to chests or belongings rather than barrels, so the lines also fit other containers.
- Consolidated the English and Chinese **Camp Community and Membership Guide**, covering exploration, admission, public supplies, personal requests, shared meals, beds and perches, local trust, outfit rewards, bonds, repairs and changing homes.

Permanent dialogue rituals still require **TrueTransfur**. Native Origin re-selection retains its existing server permission and gamerule requirements.
