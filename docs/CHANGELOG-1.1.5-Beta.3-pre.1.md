# Changed: Synergy 1.1.5-Beta.3-pre.1

Preview release since **1.1.5-Beta.2-hotfix.2**.

#### Species and Forms

* **Latex Spidtaur**: Added the subterranean **Latex Spidtaur** form naturally spawning in Lush Caves. Features charged jumping, taur saddle riding, aviator gear compatibility, native clothing support, and fully grounded sleeping poses on beds.
* **Partial Pure White Latex Wolf**: Added the **Partial Pure White Latex Wolf** (pure_white_latex_wolf_partial) with seamless classic and slim player skin blending, adapted glowing-eye layers, Pure White vision and adaptation integration, native Origin selection, spawn egg, crafting recipe, and direct speech dialogue support.

#### Communities and World Generation

* **Lush Cabin underground community and Caretaker**: Added the **Lush Cabin** subterranean household in Lush Caves, led by the **Caretaker** alongside five cabin residents. Features complete English, Simplified Chinese, and Russian branching dialogue trees, shared and private storage ownership, and **Lush Cabin Explorer Maps** obtainable from watchtowers and lush-cave dungeon chests.
* **Glow berry harvesting for camp residents**: Built-in community and camp residents now reliably gather ripe glow berries from overhead vines without disturbing or mining adjacent cave ores.
* **Clearer camp leave warnings**: Updated departure confirmation dialogues across all five community leaders (Weaver, Matriarch, Archivist, Pathfinder, and Caretaker) to explicitly clarify that leaving a household revokes any borrowed reversible camp transformation.
* **Updated Desert Refuge disc log**: Replaced the optical disc found within the Desert Refuge stasis bay with the revised **Subject X-1221** laboratory incident report.

#### Player Outposts

* **Guided camp Build mode**: Added an interactive **Build** button in the Player Outpost management screen. Players can enter an in-world placement mode, cycle facility palettes (storage, bed, rest point, campfire, composter), place blocks directly drawn from inventory or linked outpost chests, and automatically link them to camp routines.

#### Companions, AI, and Compatibility

* **Follower hazard avoidance and rescue**: Bonded companions and social followers now pathfind around fire, lava, and dangerous blocks. If trapped inside hazards or catching fire, they quickly teleport to safe ground while automatically extinguishing flames.
* **Native pet menu and suit release fixes**: Separating from voluntary companion suit-ups no longer triggers emergency-rescue cooldowns, ensuring native Shift + empty-hand pet interactions (such as Changed Addon's Experiment 002 re-merge) remain accessible. Suit carrier physics and collision now cleanly reset upon dismounting.
* **Separation of combat holds and friendly hugs**: Entering friendly-hug mode alone no longer freezes regular combat grabs against datapack humanoids; only confirmed active hugs hold targets peacefully.
* **Reputation-aware rival dialogue**: Faction creatures kept neutral through positive player reputation no longer greet or react to pats with rival-hostile voice cues.
* **3D Skin Layers first-person synchronization**: Added first-person arm model synchronization with **3D Skin Layers**, ensuring partial sleeve layers and player skin dimensions display properly in first person alongside third-person torso and thigh layer meshes.
