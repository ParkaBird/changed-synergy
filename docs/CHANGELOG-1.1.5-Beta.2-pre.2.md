# Changed: Synergy 1.1.5-Beta.2-pre.2

Changes since **1.1.5-Beta.2-pre.1**.

### NPCs and local communities

- Applied the supplied Balanced Edition and continuation to 251 English, Chinese and Russian entries, preserving unprovided writing and all admission checks. Standardized Chinese camp wording on 社区. Reworked permanent-confirmation pacing and localized the Weaver’s Chinese label as 织匠.

- Added a dedicated **Community Supplies** double chest beside the treehouse, hideout and observatory campfires. Resident food, donations and gathered proceeds use this reserve exclusively; opened loot chests remain separate, and full reserves leave cargo with its carrier.
- Joining a household introduces its authored residents with one affection point each, once per individual. Current campmates receive a separate roster badge. Reduced ordinary residents' roaming margin to six blocks while retaining nearby gathering and hunting; Chiefs keep their building boundaries.
- Added fixed characters to individual structures: the calm, polite **Weaver**, protective, polite **Matriarch**, curious, polite **Archivist** and the green laboratory's curious, polite **Researcher**. They retain their authored clothes, home boundaries and personal relationships. Chief is assigned only to authored characters.
- Added English, Chinese and Russian branching conversations based on each character's own journals and notes. Their third background topic unlocks after the first two are completed. All four speak learned human language; private thoughts still require the existing understanding ability. Replaced the three Chiefs' ambient welcome, care, organizing and warning lines with the supplied dialogue.
- Applied the supplied Chinese and Russian localization to all four NPC conversation trees, replies and twelve Chief ambient lines. Emotional emphasis follows the translated phrases, while current names, branch conditions and authored page beats are retained.
- Chiefs represent their own households. Each camp has independent local trust and membership, separate from regional faction reputation, birth background and personal affection. Human, already-transformed, reversible and permanent admission routes grant 0/2/5/8 local trust; recorded reward history prevents repeat farming. Members share public stores and available beds while marked personal containers remain private; story media are freely readable.
- Expanded the treehouse, hideout and observatory into local households with real supply donations, shared resident requests, witnessed harvest-and-replant help and invitations to eat together. Residents can approach and ask to talk without opening a screen; ordinary language and telepathy rules remain in force, with Chiefs able to explain requests. Declining or staying away carries no upkeep penalty.
- Household dealings now change local trust rather than regional faction reputation. Public-store transfers are recorded to prevent recycling borrowed items for donation rewards. Request hand-ins revalidate the held item and available space; one shared request can be completed only once. Changing homes requires confirmation, existing members can discuss form changes, and old local scores and ritual history are retained.
- Permanent transformation requires TrueTransfur and a second spoken confirmation. It shares Native Origin's persistent-body initialization, TT control permissions and reversal protection, remembers later permanent form changes and restores the current body after login or respawn. Recovery respects temporary suits, instinct bodies and active rebirth. Reversible rituals remain reversible with TT installed, and successful rituals play the form's native transformation sound.
- Fixed characters return twenty server-game minutes after confirmed death or disappearance, retaining identity, relationships, offences and conversation progress. Returns require loaded storage and a safe home position. Duplicate bodies and repeatable default-clothing drops are prevented. Ordinary affection remains available; bonding, following and body takeover are closed for these characters.
- Built conversations around the hand-drawn social panel and each speaker's species colors. The speaker and local trust occupy the title strip; the dialogue box stays below the reply list, with aligned controls and the page/choice counter above the replies. Text is larger, the frame adapts to each passage, and revealing replies does not move the body.
- Reworked emotional staging throughout all four conversation trees without changing their prose. Local emphasis, pauses, speaking speed, color and restrained motion follow each character's reactions. Understood thoughts lose their outer parentheses, keep a steady strike line and whole-sentence fade, and can carry their own emotional accents. Forty-two authored page turns across all three languages follow changes of idea; small-screen overflow prefers complete sentences and clauses. Reduced UI Motion retains colors and timing while removing spatial effects.

### Eagle Owl and bird perches

- Added the playable mountain **Latex Eagle Owl**, with natural spawns, transfur items and an archive entry. It has slightly stronger Crow attributes, four extra jumps with wing beats, gliding, the existing Wing Flap ability when Addon Plus is installed, toggleable night vision, 270-degree head tracking and responsive ear tufts.
- Eagle Owls see in monochrome, including first-person hands. Color returns under a full moon or with lowered Aviator goggles; a client setting controls the monochrome effect. Corrected eye flicker and inventory-head orientation.
- Airborne Owl grabs extend the talons forward, acquire eligible contacted targets and draw caught targets back beneath the body. Targets are carried face-down by the back, with smooth transitions into an ordinary hold on landing. Normal/friendly target restrictions and independent wing animation remain in effect.
- Added standing and horizontal two-block bird perches. The stand sleeps one bird; the rail sleeps two at opposite ends. Both face the placing player and use the supplied upright sleep poses. Corrected transparent decorations, beam ends, the rail item icon, sleeping height and name-label placement.
- Perches can be bound as player-camp beds and used by eligible residents of natural camps, with available perches preferred over ordinary beds. Unassigned wild creatures do not seek them out. Eagle Owls sleep by day and Crows by night. Perch sleep uses third person and restores the previous view on waking.
- Fixed Owl head rotation being overwritten by human-model animation adapters during movement, crouching, teleporting or view changes. Applied the supplied closed-eye textures and hide Synergy eye overlays while sleeping.

### Clothing and models

- Added the **Trailcloth Outfit**, **Aviator Outfit** and redesigned **Ranger Outfit**, with the supplied models and item icons, recipes and native clothing behavior. Trailcloth and Aviator can occupy either inner or outer clothing slots. New Hyenas and Owls have a configurable 25% chance to wear their respective outfits.
- Aviator goggles can be raised or lowered by right-clicking the equipped outfit, with native style sounds. Its recipe uses amethyst shards. Juveniles wear only goggles; juvenile Ranger wearers retain only the raised headband. Tail, legless and taur variants use the appropriate upper sections; Trailcloth excludes juvenile and pure feral bodies.
- Updated female Muntjac and Spotted Hyena to the supplied curved-body models without added chest protrusions. Muntjac uses slim arms; Hyena keeps its sturdy build. All three outfits have fitted Synergy female styles, while Changed and addon female forms retain their original feminine cuts. Improved classic/slim human fitting and native shirt, lab-coat, armor and first-person sleeve compatibility.
- Redesigned female Hyena Trailcloth while retaining its original practical style, joined the torso sections and moved the trim to the upper garment edge. Connected the back strap on non-hyena wearers. Removed stray texture pixels and hidden torso caps that showed through trousers, and corrected Aviator chest clipping on native female forms.
- With Curios installed, player and creature clothing uses genuine Curios slots and storage, replacing the original clothing interface. Existing clothes migrate on load; anatomy restrictions, clothing conflicts, right-click style changes and visibility toggles remain active. The original interface remains available without Curios.

### Structures and camp life

- Moved the three community reserves to the supplied locations and marked the specified personal containers. Witnessed private searching gives warnings and repeated-use trust penalties without starting combat. Lecterns and other readable story media are freely accessible.
- Fixed a server crash when teleporting away from sleeping camp residents near a section boundary. Tracking loss no longer wakes or repositions them inside the entity-section iterator, and does not schedule a duplicate fixed NPC.

- Camp residents retain their home and dialogue when bonded, with both roster badges. Public requests use the community reserve; personal requests go directly to the requesting resident. Members at local trust 40 can claim one household outfit from their Chief.
- Registered reserve chests regain their public role when replaced at the original positions. Residents recognize suitable player-placed beds and perches inside their structure. Replaced the hideout’s six carpet bedrolls with orange beds.

- Added forest **Treehouses** and **Watchtowers**, plus the snowy mountain **Observatory Camp**, using the supplied structures by Crossader. Treehouses and watchtowers generate in ordinary forests with supported foundations, protected building space and a terrain transition around the clearing. Watchtowers contain loot without creature residents.
- The treehouse has a Ranger-clad Silk Moth Chief, Forest-population residents and three Bees in the hive beneath the roots. Applied the completed structure edits, changed the loom-side double chest to shepherd loot, continued long journal passages without shortening them and corrected the clipboard's species to Latex Silk Moth.
- Observatories generate as rare landmarks in **Snowy Slopes** and **Grove**, with supported foundations and a snowy clearing. The orange Original-eyed Archivist starts in his bedroom beside the lectern, wearing Aviator clothing. Two Yuins, a Blue Wolf and a Pink Wyvern share the camp; the supplied journal, note, passwords and loot remain intact.
- Treehouses use woodland-mansion placement spacing and hideouts use ocean-monument spacing. Added real explorer maps to suitable watchtower, tropical-camp and vanilla biome-associated chest loot, including mountain sources for observatories. Missing destinations produce no blank map, and destination searches are bounded to avoid long stalls.
- Corrected hyena-camp and hideout foundation heights, filled the missing ground beside the hideout well and removed the draft-book chests beside that well and the treehouse campfire. Small camps start with two males; hideouts start with four males and one female. Only hideouts replenish the authored female resident; tropical wilderness can still spawn either sex.
- The Matriarch starts in the orange-bed room wearing Trailcloth, with copper-orange Slim eyes. The Weaver has mist-blue Tall eyes. The laboratory Wanderer has mint-green Original eyes, a lab coat and a red necktie. Existing fixed characters receive these presets without losing their identities. Ordinary structure residents keep persistent randomized native eyes.
- Natural-camp residents collect and store supplies, harvest and replant crops, hunt nearby wild prey, share campfire meals and sleep in their structure's available beds. They protect livestock, stay near home and do not build another outpost. Laboratory residents retain their activity boundaries without taking on gathering or hunting duties.
- Camp and outpost storage reactions require a nearby awake eyewitness with clear sight of an actual matching container opening. Natural-camp residents now act on witnessed offences and return after pursuit, with separate warning windows for each camp. Existing wild-outpost guard action rules are retained.
- Creatures that open ordinary doors can also open wooden trapdoors and fence gates along their route, closing them once clear. Iron trapdoors remain redstone-operated. Resting creatures prefer carpet over bare ground, below usable seats.

### Gameplay and interface fixes

- Applied the hand-drawn pixel archive artwork, aligned localized cover lettering to its title frame and removed the two right-hand corner plates. Origin data, dynamic previews and selection controls are retained.

- Fixed organic creatures treating neutral transformed outsiders as hostile targets, which could trigger pursuit and secondary transfur despite their social relation. Provocation, betrayal and genuinely hostile standing retain their existing consequences.
- Female Spotted Hyenas have slightly higher health, movement speed and jump strength than males. Corrected female Hyena eyes disappearing when looking down and applied the updated eyebrow textures.
- Hyenas can chew bones for food and eight seconds of a random level-I benefit: Speed, Haste, Strength, Resistance or Regeneration. They can eat leather for brief Resistance and suitable positive brewing ingredients for their matching effects. Negative ingredients provide food without harmful effects; rotten flesh restores six food points without Hunger. Companions and camp meals can use stored bones.
- Unified all seven Synergy creatures' common drops with White Latex Goo and Latex Base, using native count and Looting behavior while retaining species materials. Replaced the Owl's missing Crow-table reference with feather drops. Swordfish cod cooks when the creature is burning.
- Added **Forgive** to transfur negotiations: it grants affection once, ends negotiation and receives a creature-spoken reply while keeping the current body. Absorbed individuals retain their identity and relationships for later separation. Addon Plus's Diffusion Syringe restores that individual; ordinary assimilation still creates a fresh creature.
- Airborne releases keep the target at the carrier's current position, clear stale interpolation and prevent reacquisition for one second. Normal and friendly grabs share this protection. Rejected Owl catches immediately clear the predicted client hold, avoiding ghost catches and snap-back releases.
- Fixed Muntjac charge input blocking creative-flight double taps.
- Fixed human-start players being unable to invite a bond after ordinary transformation. Existing relationship, offering and ownership checks remain. TT Instinct interactions use the controlled body's position and the player's offering inventory; an optional setting also permits human-start players to enter Instinct after transformation.
- Native-origin players can occasionally make animal calls while chatting, with a separate setting and server cooldown. Global and local role chat use the controlled body's position.
- Wheels with more than eight options scroll continuously through fading incoming and outgoing icons. Shift+scroll switches paired wheels; smaller wheels retain plain scrolling to switch. Removed arrow-key paging and shortened the control hints.
- Fixed the right-side social wheel sliding through the center when opening **Talk about this transfur**. Fixed native-origin archive pages briefly flashing back before settling. Updated several Chinese advancement titles.
- Guards proactively defend against visible nearby hostile monsters. Player-camp guards watch around their own positions and honor their assigned duty even when companion settings require the owner to fight first. Workers keep their existing self-defense rules.
- Fixed social-awareness scans clearing excluded Addon Plus bosses' combat targets and navigation. Fixed Synergy's development and data-generation startup failures caused by production-only field and injection names.

Servers and clients must both update to Beta.2-pre.2.
