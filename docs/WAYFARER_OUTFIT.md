# Wayfarer Outfit / 沙行装

One item, `changed_synergy:desert_nomad_outfit`, provides both the ordinary desert outfit and its Pathfinder cut. The wearer's current Jerboa body selects the Pathfinder appearance automatically; it is not a second craftable garment.

## Wearing it

Supported adult bodies use the fitted upper outfit and, where appropriate, leg wraps. Tail, legless and taur bodies omit the lower section. Four-arm and original female bodies use their corresponding cuts. Humans use fitted classic or slim skin arms. Juveniles keep the supplied complete juvenile design, including its blanket, wraps and waterskin, rather than only a headband.

The garment can occupy an available native inner Body or outer Full Body clothing slot. Juveniles can also use the head slot. The headwear and applicable legwear reserve conflicting native slots, and the same outfit cannot be equipped twice. With Curios installed, the same equipment rules apply through Curios-backed clothing slots. The headband is fitted above the high eye style.

## Crafting and rewards

```text
SGS
LWL
 L
```

S is string, G copper ingot, L leather and W orange wool: two string, one copper ingot, three leather and one orange wool. The recipe is `data/changed_synergy/recipes/desert_nomad_outfit.json`; the last row has a leather in its center.

Eligible Desert Refuge members can receive this outfit once at 40 local trust. Small-camp residents use their single 25% clothing roll. Neither mechanism creates a separate Pathfinder item. See [community membership](CAMP_MEMBERSHIP_GUIDE.md) and [Jerboa](LATEX_JERBOA.md).
